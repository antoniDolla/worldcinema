A PARTNERSHIP, NOT A PROMISE

Clear terms.
Creative independence.

Any future film partnership would be discussed individually with the relevant rights holder. We aim to prioritise non-exclusive arrangements, transparent reporting and fair compensation.

Rights, territories, licensing periods, permitted uses and revenue-sharing terms would all need to be agreed in writing before a film could be included. We never include a film without permission.
