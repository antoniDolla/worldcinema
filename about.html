ABOUT WORLDCINEMA

Stories travel.
So should cinema.

WorldCinema is an independent project focused on discovering, celebrating and eventually helping distribute independent films across borders.

OUR MISSION

Make room for
more voices.

So much remarkable cinema struggles to find an audience beyond its home country. WorldCinema wants to help change that — through curation, filmmaker relationships and, over time, responsible distribution partnerships.

We believe artistic quality and cultural perspective matter more than popularity alone.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Discovery. Fairness.
Creative independence.

We want to build with filmmakers, treat rights seriously, communicate transparently and make international cinema easier to discover.
